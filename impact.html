Donation Records

Where the support goes, exhibition by exhibition.

ABST is run entirely on donations and in-kind support from families, partner schools, and local sponsors. This page tracks what came in and where it went, year by year.

2026

3rd Exhibition · CRADLE: 우리, 그 너머

ABST's third annual exhibition ran at the Jeju International Peace Centre from May 16 to May 31, 2026, supported by Jeju Special Self-Governing Province and a Seogwipo City culture & arts grant. Goods sales raised ₩2,600,000 and direct donations added ₩2,400,000, for a total of ₩5,000,000 raised. A follow-up installation, ABST Story Wall, has kept the exhibition's work on view at Tamna Comprehensive Welfare Center for the Disabled through August 20, 2026. Messages left by visitors and donors during the exhibition are archived below — click to open the full version.

₩5,000,000 Total raised
1,568 Visitors
10 Partner schools

2025

2nd Exhibition · The Flatland Dream

ABST's second annual exhibition ran at Yak Village from May 17 to June 8, 2025, in partnership with 17 collaborating artists. Sales of exhibition goods (crystal paperweights, notebooks, t-shirts, and memo pads) raised ₩1,200,000, which Yak Village's support made possible by covering production costs. The full amount was donated on September 12, 2025 to Seogwipo City Comprehensive Welfare Center for the Disabled, designated to support disabled youth pursuing careers as artists.

₩1,200,000 Total raised
17 Artists supported
100% Donated to Seogwipo Welfare Center
