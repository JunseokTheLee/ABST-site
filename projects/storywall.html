Follow-Up Exhibition · 2026

ABST Story Wall

After "CRADLE: 우리, 그 너머" closed at the Jeju International Peace Centre, ABST didn't want its stories to end with the show. Story Wall takes the exhibition's printed catalogue apart page by page, frames each spread, and hangs it as a small gallery in community spaces — first at Tamna Comprehensive Welfare Center for the Disabled, from June 18 through August 20, 2026.

Because the catalogue pairs every artwork with its description, Story Wall isn't just a wall of images — it's a wall of stories. Visitors move through it the way they would a picture book, one framed page at a time, reading the collaboration behind each piece as they view it.

The Format

Rather than re-transporting original paintings, Story Wall reuses CRADLE's exhibition catalogue — separating its pages, framing each one, and mounting them along a wall so image and description are read together, like a picture book.

The Host

The first run is installed at Tamna Comprehensive Welfare Center for the Disabled, running June 18 through August 20, 2026. Future runs are open to any partner school or institution with the wall space to host one.

On the Wall

Framed catalogue panels from CRADLE: 우리, 그 너머 — images are being added as each installation is documented.
