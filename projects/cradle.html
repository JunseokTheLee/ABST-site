Exhibition Theme · 2026

Cradle

"Cradle" was the theme of ABST's third annual exhibition, an exploration of the fragile structures humans build around themselves to feel safe: systems of belief, stereotypes, and patterns. Inspired by the symbolic undercurrents of Kurt Vonnegut's novel Cat's Cradle, the theme examines the human tendency to cling to comforting narratives that dissuade us from truth, or at least from progress.

More broadly, Cradle deals in illusions and lies — the kind that comfort us simply because they are convenient and accessible — and this exhibition was built to encourage doubt of all, and above all. To hold certainty in anything is to shut one's heart behind bars, foreclosing both tolerance and perspective. A solidified, doubtless worldview is a quiet despotism, one that never has to ask who its comforting rhetoric serves, and who it conveniently neglects.

The exhibition's tagline, "우리, 그 너머," carries a dual meaning. In Korean, 우리 can mean a physical enclosure — a fence, a cage — or "we," a collective identity. The phrase points toward moving beyond both at once: past the boundaries placed around us, and past the frameworks we've already built for ourselves.

Through Cradle, ABST asked its viewers to sit with the idea that the narratives they know best are only products of their own perception — not universal, not necessarily shared — and to carry that doubt into everything. Above all, one question: is your view a window into the world, or a cage you have learned to look through?

The Collaboration

Cradle paired 12 disabled artists with 24 student artists from four partner schools, resulting in over twenty pairs of works created and exhibited in direct response to one another.

The Space

The exhibition ran at the Jeju International Peace Center from May 15 to May 31, 2026, marking ABST's third annual exhibition.
