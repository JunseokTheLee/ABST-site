Invited Exhibition · 2025

Beyond the Everyday

In December 2025, ABST was invited into "일상 그 너머의 기억과 변화전" ("Memories and Change Beyond the Everyday"), a province-wide exhibition bringing together disabled students, parents, and teachers from across Jeju Island. Held at the Jeju Culture & Arts Center's third exhibition hall from December 6 to 11, the show gathered 84 participants — 76 students, 3 parents, and 5 teachers — around 64 individual and collaborative works. The invitation came from the exhibition's organizing teacher, who had seen The Flatland Dream earlier that year and wanted ABST's digital format brought into the show.

For the exhibition, ABST built a Virtual Art Museum housing all 64 works on display, designed by ABST's student director Junseok Lee and shaped around the exhibition's own concept — visitors could move through the gallery with a controller much as they would at an ABST show. Alongside it, two collaborative pieces by ABST member artists, originally created for The Flatland Dream, were shown again as invited work.

It was the first time ABST's virtual-museum format was built for an exhibition it didn't organize itself — proof the format could travel beyond ABST's own shows and serve a larger, island-wide integrated art exhibition.
