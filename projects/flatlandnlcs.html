Flatland Dream — NLCS Jeju

Project image — placeholder

NLCS Jeju was the founding partner school behind The Flatland Dream, ABST's 2025 exhibition. Ten NLCS Jeju students took part as artists, with the exhibition's planning, execution, and media art created by Lee Jun-seok.

Student artists: Lee Jun-seok, Kang Min-jae, Kim Sung-hyun, Kim Eung-gu, Choi Ha-rang, Choi Si-on, Lee Jung-min, Lim Da-hye, Yang Si-woo, and Yang Yeo-rim.

Their work was shown alongside pieces by seven autistic youth art creators, with select works displayed side by side as collaborative pairs — the core of ABST's approach to co-creation rather than simple representation.
