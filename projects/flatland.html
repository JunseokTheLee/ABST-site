Exhibition Theme · 2025

The Flatland Dream

"The Flatland Dream" was the theme of ABST's second annual exhibition. The title served as a reminder that a work of art is merely one aspect of an individual's projection onto a flat canvas, representing the landscape of ideas that defines a person's personality.

The theme was inspired by Edwin Abbott's novel Flatland: A Romance of Many Dimensions, which depicted a strictly divided two-dimensional world based on the number of sides an individual possessed — a satire of society's attempts to classify and separate people by rigid criteria.

As with every ABST exhibition, the physical show at the Jeju International Peace Centre was paired with a virtual interactive space built around the same theme: columned galleries, a starlit corridor overlooking the earth, and quieter outdoor scenes built from the artists' own work, letting visitors explore the exhibition beyond its three-week run.
