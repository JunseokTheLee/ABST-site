Exhibition Theme · 2024

Abstraction

"Abstraction" was the theme of ABST's very first exhibition, staged at NLCS Jeju's main building in June 2024. Rather than hanging paintings on a wall, the show unfolded entirely as a digital, controller-navigated virtual museum — a space visitors moved through rather than simply looked at.

The concept, the virtual space, its soundtrack, and the on-site installation were all designed and built by founder Lee Jun-seok, who treated the museum itself as an artwork tuned to the exhibition's theme: its layout, pacing, and the background music he composed all traced back to the idea of abstraction.

Eleven NLCS Jeju students exhibited alongside two autistic youth artists, Kim Yong-won and Son Woo-jin, whose work was given its own dedicated space within the museum. To keep visitors engaged with each piece, ten locked boxes were hidden around the exhibition, each opened by solving a quiz tied to a specific artwork — by the end of the run, visitors had cracked all ten and emptied every box of its reward.

The Concept

Abstraction was ABST's founding project — the first time a digital exhibition space was designed as an artwork in its own right rather than a backdrop for one. It set the template every show since has followed: a physical or virtual venue built around a single theme.

Visitor Engagement

The ten lock-box quizzes turned looking into searching, pushing visitors to study each artwork closely enough to find its answer — the starting point for ABST's approach of pairing exhibitions with participatory, digital programming.
