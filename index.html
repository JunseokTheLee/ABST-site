What is ABST?

ABST — Art Beyond Some Thresholds — brings together high school art students and local autistic/disabled youth artists to build exhibitions rooted in real collaboration. Rather than simply displaying work side by side, artists partner directly, moving beyond representation toward genuine co-creation.
