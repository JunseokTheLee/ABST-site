Building the 2024 Virtual Interactive Space

Post image — placeholder

Every ABST exhibition pairs its physical gallery with a virtual interactive space built around the same annual theme — a way to promote a wider range of artistic forms beyond what fits on a wall, and to let the exhibition reach people who can't visit Jeju in person.

For our first annual exhibition, that meant designing an explorable digital gallery: columned halls housing paintings and sculpture, a starlit corridor overlooking the earth, and a quieter outdoor scene built entirely from the artists' own work. Visitors could walk through the space at their own pace, the same way they would move through a physical show.

The virtual space isn't a replacement for the exhibition — it's an extension of it, letting the same collaborative works exist in a second medium. That approach carried forward into 2025's The Flatland Dream, and will continue into our third annual exhibition in May 2026.
