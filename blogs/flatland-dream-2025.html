The Flatland Dream — 2025 Exhibition Recap

Post image — placeholder

Our second annual exhibition, The Flatland Dream, ran from May 17 through June 3, 2025. The title was a reminder that a work of art is only one projection of a person's inner landscape onto a flat canvas — inspired in part by Edwin Abbott's Flatland: A Romance of Many Dimensions, a novel that satirized how society classifies and separates people by rigid criteria.

The exhibition brought together 17 artists: 10 students from NLCS Jeju and 7 autistic youth art creators. Together they produced 20 works — 18 paintings, a video installation using a beam projector, and a digital museum experience piece. Two collaborative works were displayed side by side, letting visitors take in both perspectives at once.

By placing work from autistic youth art creators alongside pieces made in direct response to them, the show became less about representation and more about genuine exchange — an opportunity to experience different worldviews and methods of expression side by side, and to find the artistic synergy that comes from that kind of collaboration.

As with previous years, the physical exhibition was paired with a virtual interactive space built around the same theme, letting the show live on beyond its three-week run at the Jeju International Peace Centre.
