Welcome to the New ABST Site

Post image — placeholder

ABST — Art Beyond Some Thresholds — now has a home online. This site will be where we share updates from our exhibitions, introduce the artists we work with, and document the collaborative process between our high school members and local autistic/disabled youth artists.

We're based on Jeju Island, with members active across four schools in the United States and South Korea. Our third annual exhibition is being planned for May 2026 at the Jeju International Peace Centre, following two successful exhibitions in 2024 and 2025. As always, this year's show will pair a physical exhibition with a virtual interactive space built around the same theme.

More updates, and more posts, are on the way as planning continues.
